# Amyloid Fibril & Fibrin Microclot Detection Methods (2026 Reference)

A sourced technical reference comparing biophysical methods for detecting cross-beta sheet **amyloid fibrils** and **fibrin amyloid microclots** (fibrinaloid microclots), including Thioflavin T (ThT) fluorescence microscopy, Congo red birefringence, electron microscopy, cryo-EM, RT-QuIC seeding assays, and standard coagulation panels (D-dimer).

## 1. Biophysical Amyloid Fibril Detection Methods Compared

Amyloid is a shared protein fold (**cross-beta sheet** architecture) rather than a single protein. Each assay targets a different physical property of that fold:

| Method | What It Detects | Typical Sample | Clinical vs. Research |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thioflavin T (ThT) Fluorescence** | Cross-beta sheet groove binding (fluorescence emission spike) | Plasma, blood smear, tissue, plate kinetics | Research & commercial observational use |
| **Congo Red + Polarized Light** | Apple-green birefringence in ordered fibrils | Tissue biopsy | Clinical standard (systemic amyloidosis) |
| **Electron Microscopy (TEM / SEM)** | Unbranched fibril width & dense matted fibrin | Purified protein, clots, tissue | Research |
| **X-Ray Fibre Diffraction** | Characteristic 4.7 Å / 10 Å cross-beta spacing | Purified fibrils | Structural biology research |
| **Cryo-EM** | Near-atomic fibril fold resolution | Extracted or synthetic fibrils | Structural biology research |
| **Seeding Assays (RT-QuIC)** | Templated conversion of normal protein into fibrils | CSF, skin, olfactory mucosa | Clinical for prion disease (CJD) |
| **Conformation-Specific Antibodies** | Soluble prefibrillar oligomers missed by ThT/Congo red | Tissue, biofluids | Mostly research |

## 2. Amyloid Fibrin Test (ThT Microscopy) vs. D-Dimer

**D-dimer** and **ThT microclot microscopy** measure different things. D-dimer is a validated clinical test; ThT microclot microscopy is a research method with no reference range:

| Test | What It Measures | Primary Clinical Use | Status |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D-Dimer** | Soluble fibrin fragments released *as clots dissolve* | Ruling out acute DVT, PE, monitoring DIC | Standard, validated |
| **PT/INR & aPTT** | Clotting cascade speed | Bleeding risk, anticoagulant monitoring | Standard, validated |
| **Fibrinogen** | Total soluble clotting protein in plasma | Bleeding disorders, DIC, inflammation | Standard, validated |
| **Amyloid Fibrin Test (ThT)** | Intact, plasmin-resistant fibrin amyloid microclots | Observational research in post-viral / inflammatory cohorts | Research-stage / observational |
